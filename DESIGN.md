# Design and audience

## Intended audience

The reference film, *The Last Coffee Ceremony*, is designed for family/community viewers interested in Ethiopian coffee ceremony, intergenerational memory, hospitality, and cultural continuity. Narration is Amharic; subtitles are English. The final film is 60–90 seconds and must work on both mobile (9:16) and widescreen (16:9).

## Creative constraints

- Use fictional characters by default; avoid recognizable real-person likenesses.
- Keep one or two characters per shot and center important subjects for vertical reframing.
- Use a single narrator, no synthetic lip-sync, gentle motion, and short narration per shot.
- Maintain culturally specific visual details through human review, not model assumptions alone.
- Human approval is required for keyframes. Music candidates are selected automatically using a documented technical rubric; the selected score can still be auditioned as part of the creative review.

## Audience tests to conduct

Record at least three viewers per round, ideally including Amharic speakers and viewers familiar with the ceremony. Ask whether the story is understandable from narration alone, whether the cultural details feel respectful/accurate, whether subtitles match the spoken meaning, and whether pacing/emotion work. Record anonymized feedback, changes made, and a before/after note here. Do not claim tests occurred until they have been conducted.
